Privacy Policy

Nagara Witch, by Endinov Games

Effective date: 20 October 2026

This policy explains what information the game Nagara Witch ("the game") uses, why, and the choices you have. Endinov Games ("we", "us") makes the game.

The short version

  • There is no sign-in and no account. We never ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • Your progress is saved on your own device. It is not sent to us.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect some device information to show and measure those ads, as described below.
  • Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details.
  • We do not sell your information.

Information saved on your device

The game keeps one save file on your device. It holds the levels you have unlocked, your stars and best scores, the weapons Amara has found, your sound and difficulty settings, the cowries in Amara's purse (including any you have bought), the outfits and weapons she owns, and whether you have bought "Remove ads". This file stays on your device and is not sent to us. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include this file in your device backup. Google handles that backup under its own terms, and you can turn it off in your device settings.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of the game shows ads provided by Google AdMob: a full-screen ad between some levels, and an optional video you may choose to watch after Amara falls, at most once in each level, to carry on from where she fell.

To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect and process information from your device. This can include your device's advertising ID, your IP address (from which an approximate location may be worked out), your device model and operating system, and how you interact with ads. Google acts under its own privacy policy:

  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

The game asks Google for ads rated no higher than Teen (T). It never asks for ads rated for mature audiences.

Where the law requires it, for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent through Google's consent form before any ad is requested. If you do not give consent, you may still see ads that are not personalised, or none at all. You can change your choice at any time under Settings, then "Ad privacy choices", where this option is shown.

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings, under Google, then Ads.

Purchases (Google Play)

The game offers these purchases through Google Play:

  • "Remove ads", a one-time purchase that switches off the between-level ads.
  • Pouches of cowries, the game's in-game currency, which can be bought more than once.
  • Three weapons (Ogun's cutlass, Oya's wind fan and the ancestors' bow), each a one-time purchase. Each can also be earned in play or bought with cowries.

Google processes every payment under its own terms and privacy policy. We receive only a confirmation that a purchase was made, which the game uses to switch off the ads, to add the cowries to Amara's purse, or to give her the weapon, and to restore "Remove ads" and bought weapons if you reinstall. We never receive your card or payment details.

Device permissions

The game asks Android for these permissions:

  • Internet and network state: to load ads and to check purchases with Google Play.
  • Advertising ID: used by Google AdMob, as described above.
  • Vibrate: for controller and phone rumble during play.

The game does not use your camera, microphone, contacts, files, or precise location.

Children

Nagara Witch is intended for adults and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information through the game, please contact us and we will help.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell your personal information and we do not share it with anyone, except that Google AdMob and Google Play receive information directly from your device as described above.

Keeping and protecting information

We do not run servers that store information about players. Information collected by Google is kept according to Google's own policies.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, or to object to its use. Because we do not hold personal information about you, requests about advertising data are best made to Google, using the links above. You are welcome to contact us with any question and we will help where we can.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at the same address and in the game, with a new effective date.
